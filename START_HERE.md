### Step 4: PLAY!
Choose from **6 exciting game modes**!

## 🎮 6 GAME MODES AVAILABLE:

### 1. 📖 **Learn Mode**
- No time pressure
- Browse all 26 letters
- Perfect for beginners

### 2. ⚡ **Practice Mode** 
- 10 seconds per question
- 10 questions
- Build confidence

### 3. 🏆 **Challenge Mode**
- 5 seconds per question
- Fast-paced action
- Test your skills

### 4. 🧠 **Memory Challenge** ⭐ NEW!
- See sign for 3 seconds
- Image disappears
- Answer from memory
- **Streak tracking!** 🔥

### 5. ⚡⚡ **Speed Run** ⭐ NEW!
- ULTRA FAST - 2 seconds!
- Expert mode
- Lightning reflexes

### 6. ❤️ **Survival Mode** ⭐ NEW!
- 3 lives system
- Unlimited questions
- How far can you go?
- Lose a life per mistake
